Program Overview

Eduford’s Business Administration program prepares students to lead in the global business environment. You’ll gain strategic insight into management, finance, marketing, entrepreneurship, and operations.

What You'll Learn

  • Financial Accounting & Management
  • Marketing Strategy & Consumer Behavior
  • Leadership & Organizational Behavior
  • Business Ethics & Law
  • Operations & Supply Chain Management
  • Entrepreneurship & Innovation

Career Opportunities

Graduates pursue careers as business analysts, marketing managers, consultants, and executives in startups, corporations, and NGOs worldwide.
